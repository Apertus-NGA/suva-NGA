# Role brief: CRAWL and ingestion

> **Status: v0 PROPOSAL (27 Nov 2026), updated after the template was read.** Owner: `<handle>`. Read `AGENTS.md`, `docs/DATA.md` and `docs/SUVA_DATA.md` first. Which date defines a quarter is OPEN (D-050): this brief follows its "while open" line.

## Mission
Produce, for Q4/2025, Q1/2026 and Q2/2026, the complete list of Federal Court rulings of each quarter, each with its metadata, its text, its **decision date** and its **publication date**, so that no relevant ruling can be lost before triage. Which of the two dates defines a quarter is OPEN (D-050). While open: crawl the Neuheiten pages of every day from **1 Oct 2025 to 15 Sep 2026** (the end date is a parameter), so that each quarter is complete under both definitions, and build each quarter's population both ways (by publication date and by decision date). D-009's publication-date window (rulings published 1 Apr to 30 Jun 2026) stays the default for the delivered Q2 list until Q-02 is answered (D-052). The window is a parameter: the template's "last 3 months" is Q-16.

## You own
`src/crawl/`, `data/public/`, the data manifests, `make crawl`. Also the reader of `judgements/` (stage S0): one parser for Suva's relevance list and the 55 summary exports, used by triage, summarise and eval, which keeps only the fields D-051 allows. And the Suva completeness check (step 7b).

## Inputs
- Daily Neuheiten pages on `search.bger.ch` (URL templates in `docs/DATA.md` §3).
- HF `voilaj/swiss-caselaw` (`data/bger.parquet`, `structure/erwaegungen_paragraphs.parquet`), pinned revision.
- The team's list of 2,096 Q2 rulings: exported from the search.bger.ch date search, 1 Apr to 30 Jun 2026, so it holds the rulings **decided** in Q2/2026 (location and provenance: `docs/STATE.md` §2).
- The datasets to build and their expected counts, measured during preparation: `docs/DATA.md` §10 (the single home of the list) and §6.
- Fallbacks: entscheidsuche.ch, opencaselaw API.
- `judgements/` (template, read-only; never moved, renamed, edited or deleted, D-040): the dockets and quarter labels of Suva's relevance list `BG_Urteile_2025_Q4_2026_Q1.xlsx`, and the dockets, decision dates and texts of the 55 summary exports. Content and counts: `docs/SUVA_DATA.md` §1-4. At ingestion keep only docket, dates, quarter label, `Text DE`, `Text FR` and the bger.ch link; drop author fields, staff codes and every intranet link (D-051). Parse the workbook's cells only, never its file properties. Quirks to survive: the list cell `458/2025` has no court prefix (it is `8C_458/2025`, through its export); `Export_8C_176_2025 3.html` is 8C_176/2025 (T29); the others are listed in `docs/SUVA_DATA.md` §3. The decision date of an export comes from its title line or from the `aza://` id of its bger.ch link (they agree in every file).

## Outputs (contract: `RulingRecord` in `src/schemas.py`)
One record per (docket, decision_date): `publication_date`, `court`, `register_letter`, `language`, `sachgebiet`, `gegenstand`, `star`, `url`, `text_source`, `text_hash`, `full_text`, `clean_length`, `considerations[]`, `parties_block`, `bench_size`, `operative_part`, `outcome`. Records are files first (Parquet or JSONL in `data/public/`), then loaded into Postgres. Plus `data/public/manifest_<quarter>.csv`, with two flags per ruling: in the quarter by publication date, in the quarter by decision date (D-050). Plus an ingestion report (counts per day, court, language; population counts per quarter under both definitions; missing texts; failures by reason). Plus the parsed `judgements/` fields and the per-docket Suva check in `data/derived/` (git-ignored while D-031 is open: they carry Suva's labels per docket); only their counts go to `results/`.

## Steps
**0. Populations first, without bger.ch** (first hour; datasets DS1 and DS2 of `docs/DATA.md` §10). Rebuild the Federal Court population by decision date in `src/crawl/population.py`, from HF `data/bger.parquet` at a pinned commit, cross-checked with entscheidsuche. Reference scripts written during preparation exist outside the repo: rewrite the logic, do not copy them. Count one row per decision: drop HF's twin rows (a `-DYYYYMMDD` suffix on `decision_id`, same date as the base row), keep two decisions that share a docket (7B_1356/2025), and deduplicate entscheidsuche files filed under several court codes. Take the subject area from `Gegenstand` (German, French, Italian), not from `legal_area`. Publication dates: HF's Neuheiten poller from 19 May 2026 (source `hf_poller`); before that, `publication_date` stays empty until a Neuheiten page gives it, and `publication_date_approx` holds the first-collection date with the quality `approx_first_seen`. Write the manifests with both quarter flags, then compare with the expected counts (1,931, 2,005 and 2,096 decisions for Q4/2025, Q1/2026 and Q2/2026): any difference goes to the lead with its dockets. Then build the Q2 union (DS2) and anti-join it with the team's 2,096 list: its decision-date part should match the list exactly.

Fetch one Neuheiten day politely from the workstation that will crawl; record the result in `results/access_test.json` and `docs/STATE.md` §3. If challenged: back off and use the fallbacks (`docs/ARCHITECTURE.md` §8); never switch networks or IP addresses to get past a challenge, never solve a challenge automatically.

   **1b. Boundary check** (D-050, due before the crawl's date range is fixed; T32 of `docs/EVAL.md` §3): find the publication dates of the 11 boundary dockets listed in D-050 (`docs/DECISIONS.md` §2 is the single home of that list: read it there, do not copy it). Crawl the boundary windows first: from each quarter boundary to about 10 weeks after it (October to mid-December 2025, January to mid-March 2026, April to mid-June 2026), because publication lags reach about 10 weeks. Use the Neuheiten pages; HF's publication date is unreliable (D-011): use it only to narrow which days to fetch first. A late-September docket absent from every page from 1 Oct 2025 was put online before that date: say so (the pages of 23 to 30 Sep 2025 date it exactly, if the team wants it). For each docket, report its decision date, its publication date and its label in Suva's list (or "summarised, not listed"). 2. **Lists:** start with a one-day vertical slice (list → records → texts), then every date from 1 Oct 2025 to 15 Sep 2026 (D-050 while open: this covers Q4/2025, Q1/2026 and Q2/2026 under both definitions, including rulings decided in a quarter and put online up to about 10 weeks after it; the end date is a parameter; pages on weekends and holidays may be empty; expected volumes: `docs/DATA.md` §6-7), GET the Neuheiten page with `lang=de`, ≥ 2 s apart, cache the raw HTML with timestamp and hash. Parse: decision date, docket, link (`aza://` id), Sachgebiet (strip and record the trailing `*` as `star`), Gegenstand; publication date = the page date; language = language of the Sachgebiet text. Store both dates on every record; never overwrite one with the other.
3. **Union** the Q2 lists with the team's 2,096 list. That list is the decision-date population of Q2 (`docs/STATE.md` §2): it should equal DS1's Q2 decision-date part, and the publication-date population adds about 580 rulings decided before 1 April (`docs/DATA.md` §6). A docket present on one side only goes to a review list with its reason. The Q2 candidate set is the union under both date definitions while D-050 is open; each record says which definition puts it in Q2.
4. **Texts:** look up each docket in HF by docket and decision date; take `full_text` and the pre-split considerations. Missing → fetch the ruling page from bger.ch (`&print=yes`, decode ISO-8859-1, extract `div.content`), split considerations yourself.
5. **Fidelity sample:** for ~20 random rulings, fetch bger.ch too and compare normalised texts; report the match rate.
6. **Parse the text** for the signals' raw material: parties block, bench (count of judges), operative part, cleaned length.
7. **Cross-check:** anti-join of 8C/9C between our lists and HF on the same period; inspect each gap.

## Rules specific to this role
- The publication date comes **only** from a Neuheiten page date: read on the page by us (source `neuheiten`), or transcribed by HF's Neuheiten poller from 19 May 2026 (source `hf_poller`; possible gaps on 21 May and 1 June 2026). Never derive a publication date, or the publication-date reading of Q2, from decision dates. A first-collection date is not a publication date: it goes to `publication_date_approx` with the quality `approx_first_seen`, and serves only estimates and the union (DS2), never a reported publication date. The decision date is stored too (from the list and the `aza://` id), and which of the two defines a quarter is D-050 (OPEN): while open, build every quarter under both definitions; D-009's publication-date window stays the default for the delivered list (D-052).
- `judgements/` is read-only (D-040). No author field, staff code or intranet link from it enters a record, a manifest, a log or any file we write (D-051).
- The star exists only in the Neuheiten list: capture it at crawl time.
- Never filter on docket prefix here: ingestion keeps every published ruling (triage decides).
- `robots.txt`: `Crawl-delay: 2`; never touch `/php/jurivoc`, `/php/taf`, `/php/azabvger`.
- Detect firewall pages (`_Incapsula_Resource`, missing results table) and back off exponentially; detect "Dokument nicht gefunden" (HTTP 200).
- TLS: prefer `search.bger.ch`; never disable certificate verification.
- Do not trust HF's `publication_date` (except the poller's dates from 19 May 2026, above), `marked_for_publication`, `legal_area`, `judges`, `outcome`.
- Key = (docket, decision_date); one docket can hold several decisions.
- Length for example rule SR8 is measured on the cleaned text, without the site menu.

## Pitfalls seen during preparation
- The firewall challenged cloud IPs after ~25 requests in 20 minutes, and an automated browser on the venue network.
- entscheidsuche had no runs from 7 May to 3 Jun 2026 and its `Index/` stops on 19 Jun 2026; it mislabels 8C/9C after 2023.
- HF considerations (`structure/`) can lag the texts (`data/`) by days.
- HF knows publication dates only from 19 May 2026; before that they are nearly always empty, or equal to the decision date and so doubtful. HF has twin rows per decision, and 6B_920/2025 carries a 2022 date on its base row. entscheidsuche files the same ruling under 2 or 3 court codes.
- Lags of 9 days to about 10 weeks between decision and publication were observed (`docs/CHALLENGE.md` §10). A ruling decided on 30 June can go online in early September, which is why D-050 runs the crawl to 15 Sep 2026. Report the lag distribution measured on the crawl: if Q2 decisions are still being put online at the end of the range, tell the lead so the end date (a parameter) can be extended.

## Done when
DS1 and DS2 rebuilt in `src/` and matching the expected counts of `docs/DATA.md` §10, or every difference explained. All three quarters ingested under both date definitions (every day from 1 Oct 2025 to 15 Sep 2026, D-050); every Q2 ruling of the union has a decision date, a publication date and a text (or a counted reason why not); the 11 boundary dockets of D-050 reported to the lead; every docket of `judgements/` found in the populations, or its absence explained, the cantonal row reported as out of reach (Q-28); manifests committed; ingestion report in `results/ingestion.json`; `make run` replays it from the snapshot.

## Hand-off
To triage: the records, the review list and the parsed relevance list (`data/derived/`). To summarise: the parsed exports (docket, dates, `Text DE`, `Text FR`, bger.ch link only, D-051). To eval: the Q4/2025 and Q1/2026 populations under both date definitions (D-050) for negatives, and the Suva check.
