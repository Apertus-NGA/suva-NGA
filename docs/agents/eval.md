# Role brief: EVALUATION, reproducibility and the model registry

> **Status: v0 PROPOSAL (27 Nov 2026), updated after the template was read.** Owner: `<handle>`. Read `AGENTS.md`, `docs/EVAL.md`, `docs/SUVA_DATA.md` and `MODELS.md` first.

## Mission
Produce the numbers that make Suva and the jury believe us: recall/F2 on a held-out labelled quarter with intervals; precision and hits at 30 (D-046); recall on the must-hit subset of rulings Suva summarised (Q-20); summary quality on an internal hold-out; verification rates, cost and latency; and a `make run` that reproduces the public part of it (D-031, D-032).

## You own
`eval/`, `results/`, `expected/`, `make eval`, `make eval-private` (needed only under D-031 option (a)), `make claims`, `docs/EVAL.md`, and the upkeep of `MODELS.md` with the lead. Also the numbers of `technical_report.md` §6 (hardware, runtime, seeds, exact commit) and §7 Evaluation, through `make claims`. The gateway probe belongs to the platform role (`docs/agents/platform.md`); you read its results.

## Steps
1. **First hour:** with the platform owner, make sure the gateway probe runs (`docs/EVAL.md` §7, `make smoke-llm`) and compute the capacity floor (`docs/ARCHITECTURE.md` §6), counting the labelled quarters and the repetitions, not only Q2. Raise D-031 (what we may commit that is derived from Suva's data, and what a public `make run` can reproduce) before the first commit of `results/`, `expected/`, a cache or a prompt fixture; its hard due is Fri 22:00, before the overnight run. With the triage owner, raise D-046 (what the hit score measures) before the scorer is built. Build the smoke set (DS7, `docs/EVAL.md` §3b) as soon as the client works, and run it before every long or overnight run.
2. **Freeze the gold:** Suva's relevance list `judgements/BG_Urteile_2025_Q4_2026_Q1.xlsx` (SHA-256 `f7146cfd…`) and the 55 summary exports (manifest of `judgements/` `aee08754…`; full values in `docs/SUVA_DATA.md` §1), read through the S0 parser (D-051), plus the populations from crawl → manifests with SHA-256, git tag. `judgements/` itself is never edited (D-040). Rebuild the evaluation sets as dataset DS6 of `docs/DATA.md` §10 (what to build, the expected counts, where each file goes); a count that differs from the expected one stops the freeze until it is explained.
   - Positives: 28 for Q4/2025 and 31 Federal Court rows for Q1/2026; the cantonal row is out of reach and reported apart, with the ceiling (31 of 32) next to the Q1/2026 recall (Q-28).
   - Negatives: every other Federal Court ruling of the quarter, under the closed-world assumption of Q-04, stated in every report. The hard negatives (accident, disability or military rulings Suva did not keep: 104 in Q4/2025, 131 in Q1/2026, `docs/DATA.md` §6) are reported apart; the threshold is placed among them.
   - Populations follow D-050. While open, build each labelled quarter by decision date (as Suva's labels suggest) and by publication date; measure on the decision-date populations and also report the publication-date reading.
   - Must-hit subset (Q-20): defined once in `docs/GLOSSARY.md` (22 rulings in Q4/2025, 10 in Q1/2026). The 23 summaries decided in Q3/2025 are in no list: a population check (T30), not a must-hit; no recall is measured on them.
   - Derived gold files (labels per docket) follow D-031: while open, `data/derived/` (git-ignored); only hashes and aggregates are committed.
   - If the labels turn out unusable (format, ambiguity, window definition), follow the plan B of D-021.
3. **Write thresholds before measuring** (`<to set>` lines in `docs/EVAL.md`), commit.
4. **Triage runs:** A0-kw and A0 to A4 on both labelled quarters by the cross-quarter protocol of `docs/EVAL.md` §2 (F2 threshold chosen on one quarter, from out-of-fold scores under option B or directly under option A; reported on the other at that frozen threshold; then swapped; headline = the cross-quarter result with both folds shown; the final refit on Q4/2025 + Q1/2026 uses the threshold policy of D-021 point 8, frozen before Q2 predictions), 2 repetitions, Wilson and bootstrap intervals, per language and court, number of rulings to read per week. Report recall and F2 at the threshold, precision and hits at 30 (D-046), and recall on the must-hit subset (Q-20). A0-kw is expected to miss the 14 positives that are pure disability rulings naming neither Suva nor accident insurance (`docs/DATA.md` §6): report how many it misses.
5. **Summary runs:** deterministic graders on all summaries; internal hold-out by time split, stratified by style: the test set is the 10 Q1/2026 exports plus the 13 German-language Q4/2025 exports (23 rulings, both styles), the few-shot and development pool the 23 Q3/2025 exports and the 9 French-language Q4/2025 exports (`docs/agents/summarise.md` step 5); never a ruling in both prompt and test; results reported per style; structure graded per style (rule-statement: no outcome sentence and no consideration reference required; narrative: facts, rule with its reference, application, outcome; style by the ruling's language while Q-30 is open); human rubric on a sample, reporting the template's two criteria apart, **style** and **legal precision**, plus structure parity between German and French (Q-27); optional open-weights judge with κ.
6. **Robustness tests** T19-T25 of `docs/EVAL.md` §3, and the template and data cases T26-T32 (8B and Hugging Face id, fresh clone, template layout, parser quirks, every docket of `judgements/` in the populations, the D-051 output test, the 11 boundary dockets of D-050).
7. **Reproducibility:** `make run` recomputes every public table in under ~5 minutes and fails on divergence with `expected/`; CI runs it on `linux/amd64` with `SERVE=0`. Until D-031 is settled, T27 and the CI `make run` replay step are marked "blocked by D-031": a fresh clone has no `data/derived/` and no replay cache, and CI has no key. Meanwhile CI runs `make check` and the `make run` stages that need no Suva-derived artefact. Under D-031 option (a) only, measurements on Suva's data run in `make eval-private` and commit aggregates only; under option (d) they run in `make eval`. While D-031 is open, they read `data/derived/` when present and regenerate it from `judgements/` otherwise, and only aggregates are committed. Record what `technical_report.md` §6 needs: hardware, runtime, seeds, exact commit.
8. **Claims:** `make claims` writes every number used in `README.md`, `technical_report.md` (§6 and §7 included), `<TeamName>_Report.pdf` (our registered team name, no spaces; Q-22) and the deck from `results/`.

## Rules specific to this role
- Apertus never judges our headline metric. A judge is open-weights, from another family, with κ on ~60 double-annotated items; otherwise human review only.
- The runner counts executed cases against the gold size; `failed` and timeouts are reported separately.
- Measurement runs bypass the replay cache (repetition index in the key); the demo cache is separate and never purged.
- Report per language; never only an aggregate.
- Gold files and per-docket results that reveal Suva's labels follow D-031 (while open: `data/derived/`). Nothing in `results/`, `expected/` or a fixture contains an author field, staff code or intranet link from `judgements/` (D-051).
- Keep `MODELS.md` exact: every runtime model id and revision, every development tool and model declared by each member.

## Done when
`results/` holds the gateway probe, triage A0-kw and A0-A4 with CIs, cross-quarter with both folds shown (recall and F2 at the threshold, precision and hits at 30, recall on the must-hit subset; on the decision-date populations, with the publication-date reading also reported), summary metrics (style and legal precision apart, structure parity, per style), robustness results (T19-T32; T27 marked "blocked by D-031" until D-031 is settled); `make run` reproduces them; `make claims` feeds `technical_report.md` §6-§7, `<TeamName>_Report.pdf` and the deck.
